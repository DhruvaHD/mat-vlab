About MAT-VLAB

Interactive Virtual Materials Testing & Analysis Laboratory designed as an educational engineering portfolio project for undergraduate Materials Science and Technology curricula.

Project Concept

MAT-VLAB was conceived as an idea by:

Dhruva H D

The concept is to create an interactive, visual virtual laboratory for Materials Science and Technology students, combining virtual experiments with manual laboratory data entry, calculations, graphs, quizzes and reports.

Project Purpose & Academic Context

Materials testing laboratories form the practical backbone of Materials Science and Metallurgical Engineering education. However, equipment such as 50 kN Universal Testing Machines (UTM), precision extensometers, and high-temperature furnaces are often resource-constrained in academic settings.

MAT-VLAB was designed to give students an authentic virtual laboratory environment where they can:

  • Prepare for physical labs: Understand testing machine anatomy, sensor principles, and specimen geometry before stepping into a workshop.
  • Simulate mechanical behavior: Observe how different crystal lattices (ferrous vs FCC aluminium/copper) deform under stress.
  • Process real experimental data: Replace cumbersome spreadsheet calculations with standardized, automated calculations of Young's Modulus ($E$), 0.2% proof stress, and UTS.
  • Generate formal engineering lab reports: Produce downloadable PDF documentation complete with publication-grade curves and observations.
Pedagogical Disclaimer

MAT-VLAB is an educational simulator and analysis tool. It does not claim to replace hands-on physical testing laboratories. Real-world laboratory experience—including physical specimen machining, mechanical calibration, safety interlocks, and physical fracture examination—is indispensable to engineering education.

System Architecture & Modularity

MAT-VLAB enforces a strict separation of concerns across presentation, business logic, engineering calculations, and persistent data layers:

Backend & Routing

Lightweight, secure Python 3 + Flask application handling modular endpoints, input validation, and RESTful JSON APIs.

Calculation Engine

Implemented in calculations/tensile.py using NumPy and Pandas to compute linear regression slopes, 0.2% offset proof stress, and trapezoidal energy integration.

Database Layer

SQLite3 relational database with relational tables for materials, experiment metadata, observation readings, calculation results, and quiz scores.

Report Generation

Programmatic PDF generation via ReportLab, dynamically rendering observation tables, metadata, and high-resolution matplotlib charts.

Reference Testing Standards
  • ASTM E8 / E8M:
    Standard Test Methods for Tension Testing of Metallic Materials (Proportional Dogbone Specimen Standards).
  • ISO 6892-1:
    Metallic materials — Tensile testing — Method of test at room temperature.
  • ASTM E83:
    Standard Practice for Verification and Classification of Extensometer Systems.
  • ASTM E9 / E18 / E23:
    Standards for compression, indentation hardness, and notched-bar impact testing.
