Hardness Test Overview

Hardness measures local resistance to permanent surface indentation.

Planned Scales:
  • Brinell (HBW): 10 mm tungsten carbide ball at 3000 kgf load
  • Rockwell (HRC/HRB): 120° diamond spheroconical indenter or 1/16" ball
  • Vickers (HV): 136° square-based diamond pyramid
Indentation optical microscope measurement module scheduled for Phase 2.
Charpy Impact Test (ASTM E23)

Evaluates notch sensitivity and dynamic fracture energy absorption by swinging a weighted pendulum hammer through a 10 mm notched specimen.

Key Concepts:
  • Potential energy conversion ($E = mgh_1 - mgh_2$)
  • Ductile-to-Brittle Transition Temperature (DBTT) curve
  • Fracture appearance: % Shear fibrous area vs cleavage crystallization
Interactive pendulum release simulation in Phase 2.
Fatigue Testing (ASTM E466)

Characterizes material degradation under fluctuating stresses well below the nominal monotonic yield strength.

Planned Simulation:
  • Basquin's equation and Wöhler S-N curve plotting
  • Fatigue limit ($\sigma_e$) identification in structural steels
  • Beach marks & striation progression visualization
High-Temperature Creep Test

Examines slow, permanent deformation of engineering components under sustained mechanical stress at elevated temperatures.

Key Parameters:
  • Larson-Miller parameter ($LMP = T(\log t_r + C)$)
  • Steady-state creep rate ($\dot{\epsilon}_{ss}$)
  • Dislocation climb & diffusional vacancy transport
