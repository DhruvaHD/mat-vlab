Key Pedagogical Capabilities

Why MAT-VLAB?

Engineered specifically for Materials Science & Technology students to bridge the gap between abstract solid mechanics theory and physical laboratory practice.

Interactive Experiments

Control testing variables, adjust specimen gauge geometry, and test different materials in an active simulation environment.

Visual Learning

Explore interactive SVG schematics of Universal Testing Machines, dogbone specimens, and metallurgical necking and fracture stages.

Manual Data Entry

Enter real observation readings obtained during physical college lab sessions or import batch laboratory CSV files directly.

Automatic Calculations

Calculate cross-sectional area, engineering stress, strain, Young's modulus ($E$), 0.2% proof stress, UTS, and % elongation instantly.

Graphs & Analysis

Generate responsive, publication-quality Plotly.js Stress-Strain curves with interactive hover telemetry and critical point markers.

Quiz & Assessment

Test your comprehension of mechanical testing standards, ASTM E8 methods, Hooke's Law, and ductile fracture with instant explanations.

Designed for Materials Science & Engineering Students

Physical laboratory testing machines such as Universal Testing Machines (UTM), Charpy impact pendulums, and creep frames are capital-intensive, high-maintenance instruments. Often, students have limited hands-on machine time, or must perform experiments in large batches where individual interactive experimentation is constrained.

MAT-VLAB addresses this challenge by providing an accessible, rigorous virtual platform for pre-lab preparation, constitutive simulation, post-lab data reduction, and automated reporting.

Academic Scope & Role

MAT-VLAB is designed as a learning companion. It does not claim to replace physical laboratories. Hands-on machine operation, physical calibration, specimen preparation, and laboratory safety protocols remain vital components of engineering education.
