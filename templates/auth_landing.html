Virtual Laboratory Features

Everything you need to complete curriculum mechanical testing coursework online.

Run interactive tensile testing simulations with live load-displacement curves, real-time necking visualizer, and instant mechanical calculations.

Input physical tensile test data directly or upload CSV files. Calculates Young's Modulus, 0.2% Offset Yield Strength, UTS, Elongation, and Toughness.

Access standard mechanical and physical properties for structural steels, aluminum alloys, titanium, brass, and copper with standard references.

Explore engineering stress-strain behavior with interactive zoom, hover telemetry, yield offset tangent lines, and fracture point identification.
