Metallurgical Conversion Notice (ASTM E140 / ISO 18265):

Hardness conversions are empirical relationships established for specific alloy families under standardized thermo-mechanical conditions. The approximation $\sigma_{uts} \approx 3.45 \times \text{HBW}$ is valid primarily for carbon and low-alloy steels. High work-hardening non-ferrous metals and austenitic stainless steels exhibit deviating relationships due to plastic strain hardening ahead of the indenter tip.
